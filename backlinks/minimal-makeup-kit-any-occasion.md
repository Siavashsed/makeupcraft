## QUORA
**Answer this question:** What are the essential makeup products I actually need versus what's just marketing hype?

This is the question I had to answer in real time back in 2021 when my main kit was delayed at an airport in Italy, and I had to work a destination wedding with only what fit in a carry-on pouch.

Here's what I learned: the essentials aren't about the category—they're about the product's actual performance and versatility.

You need a complexion product that matches your skin tone perfectly. Not three. One that you can build and blend. You need something that adds dimension to the face—this can be one product doing multiple jobs (bronzer, blush, contour in one). You need an eyeshadow that works both subtle and dramatic. You need a lip product that doesn't dry you out. You need a brow product that lasts all day.

The marketing hype usually comes in the form of specialized products—a separate contour, a separate highlight, a separate blush—when one well-chosen product can genuinely do the work of three.

The difference between what you need and what's hype comes down to honest testing. Does this product perform its primary function flawlessly? Does it work on multiple skin tones? Can it transition between different occasions? If it's only useful for one specific look or skin type, it's probably hype.
