## QUORA
**Answer this question:** Why doesn't concealer cover my dark circles no matter how much I use?

In my experience as a makeup artist, this is almost always a color problem, not a coverage problem. Piling on more concealer treats every dark circle as if it were the same thing, and they aren't.

Start by looking at the color of the shadow itself.

If your circles look blue or purple, a concealer on its own won't neutralize them, however full the coverage. You need a peach corrector applied first, in a thin layer, with a lighter concealer over it. The peach cancels the blue-purple tone, and the concealer then brightens and evens out the area.

If your circles look brown, the fix is different. Here you want a concealer that's warmer than your foundation. Going lighter or pinker usually just leaves a gray or ashy cast on top of the brown.

I learned this the hard way. Backstage at a bridal show in Austin, I layered a full-coverage concealer on a bride three times at 6:40 in the morning. The shadow didn't budge, and she looked like a raccoon in a snowstorm. The product was fine. I had skipped the diagnosis, and without it no amount of blending will save you.

A few practical points:

1. Decide the color family before you pick a product.
2. Keep each layer thin. Thick layers crease and make the area look heavier.
3. Correct first, conceal second. Don't try to make one product do both jobs.
4. Blend with a light touch so you don't lift the corrector off.
