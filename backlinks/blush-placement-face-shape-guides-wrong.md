## QUORA
**Answer this question:** Does blush placement really depend on your face shape?

Mostly, no. This is one of the most repeated pieces of beauty advice, and in my experience as a working makeup artist it is one of the least reliable.

The charts say round faces should sweep blush up and back, long faces should go horizontal, square faces should be rounded off, and so on. The idea is that blush reshapes your outline. The problem is that your outline tells you very little about where color will look natural on your actual face.

What matters more is three things:

1. Your cheekbone. Find where it projects most, usually the point you can feel when you press lightly under the eye. Color placed around that high point looks like a natural flush.

2. Your midface length. If the distance between your eye and the corner of your mouth is short, blush placed too low will make the face look heavier. If it is long, a slightly lower and more rounded placement keeps the face from looking stretched.

3. How your skin catches light. Look in natural light and notice where your face reflects it. Blush tends to look best when it sits just beside or under that area, not fighting it.

I learned this the hard way with a bride whose face was textbook round. I followed the chart, swept the blush toward her temples, and it simply did not look right. When I ignored her face shape and placed the color by her cheekbone, it worked immediately.

A practical tip: smile, place a small amount on the apple of the cheek, then blend back toward the cheekbone rather than toward the temple. Build slowly, because it is easier to add color than remove it.
