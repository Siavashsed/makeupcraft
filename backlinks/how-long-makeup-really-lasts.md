## QUORA
**Answer this question:** How long does makeup actually last after you open it?

Less time than most people think, and the date printed on the box isn't the one that matters. That date is for the sealed product. Once you open it, how you store and handle it decides how long it stays safe.

These are the timelines I use in my own kit as a professional makeup artist:

- Mascara and liquid eyeliner: about 3 months
- Liquid foundation: roughly 12 months
- Pressed powders (blush, bronzer, eyeshadow): 2 years or more

Mascara has the shortest life for a reason. It's wet, it sits in a dark tube, and the wand goes from your lashes back into the tube every day. Those are close to ideal conditions for bacteria, right next to your eyes. Pumping the wand makes it worse because it pushes air in and dries out the formula.

Powders last longest because they hold very little water, and bacteria need moisture to grow.

Signs that something has gone off:
- A sour or strange smell (the most reliable sign)
- Foundation that separates or has a watery layer
- Mascara that is clumpy, dry or flaky
- Stinging or itching when you put it on

In March 2022 I opened a bride's personal kit in a hotel room in Bologna and found a liquid liner that smelled faintly of sour milk. She had no idea how old it was. That's the usual story, because nobody keeps track.

What shortens these timelines: keeping products in a steamy bathroom, leaving them in a hot car, dipping fingers into creams, and sharing, especially eye products.

The simplest fix is a strip of tape on each product with the date you opened it. I wrote a longer breakdown of each product type and the habits that make it go off faster here: https://siavashsed.github.io/makeupcraft/how-long-makeup-really-lasts/

When you're unsure about anything that touches your eyes, replace it. Mascara is cheap, and an eye infection is not.
