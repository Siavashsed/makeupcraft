## QUORA
**Answer this question:** How do I find my undertone and match my foundation correctly?

This is the question I get asked most, and I'm glad you're asking it—because most people are given incomplete answers.

The traditional "check your veins" or "gold vs silver jewelry" tests do tell you something, but they're not the full picture. Your undertone has multiple layers: there's the base undertone (warm, cool, or neutral), but also depth and saturation, and these factors shift how a foundation will actually appear on your skin.

Here's what actually matters:

First, undertone is about the pigments beneath your skin's surface. This is separate from your skin tone depth—you can be fair with warm undertones or deep with cool undertones. They're independent variables.

Second, even if you know you're warm-toned, there are warm undertones that are peachy versus golden versus red-based. These require different foundation undertones to match.

Third, the saturation of your undertone matters. Some people have very obvious, saturated undertones; others have muted ones. A foundation that's perfect for saturated skin tones will look off on muted skin, even if the underlying warm or cool direction is right.

The real test involves observing how different foundation undertones sit on your skin in natural light—not just matching to a single characteristic.
